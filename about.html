About us

Built by people who have done the work

IndiSolution is an Indian technology firm that builds custom software, automation and AI solutions for organisations and businesses.

Why we exist

Our team has spent years working inside government and armed-forces style organisations and businesses. We have maintained the registers, prepared the returns, waited for the approvals and faced the audits.

So we understand their procedures, audit requirements, chain of approvals and security concerns first-hand. Too often, the software offered to such organisations ignores all of this — and ends up unused while the paper files carry on.

We build tools that fit how these organisations actually work: the same steps, the same approvals, the same reports — only faster, clearer and fully traceable.

What we believe

  • Understand before building. The process comes first; the software follows it.
  • Security is not optional. Access control, approvals and audit trails are built in from day one.
  • Simple beats clever. If staff need a long manual, we have not done our job.
  • Data belongs to the client. Always exportable, never shared or sold.
  • Stay for the long run. Appointments change and rules change; we keep supporting.
