The problem

Every new design needs a photoshoot you can't always afford.

Models, studios and editing for each design or colourway add up quickly, especially for print-on-demand and small-batch brands. The studio creates new imagery from your own product references, so you can show more of your range.

Who it is for
  • Fashion labels and boutiques
  • D2C brands
  • Print-on-demand sellers
  • E-commerce agencies
Capabilities

What it does

  • Upload product images and reference assets
  • Choose product type, material, model, background and visual style
  • On-model, flat-lay, ghost-mannequin, product-only and lifestyle images
  • Product artwork with print-placement instructions
  • Images for listings, campaigns and social media
  • Saved creative briefs, generation jobs and output assets
The benefit

More imagery, less shoot time.

Recurring product-content work becomes a managed workflow — and you get more options to test in listings and ads.

Peepal Studio, the workflow behind this service, was developed for our own brand, IndiPeepal, to create product imagery for its catalogue and ads.

Made to fit

What we customise

  • Your brand's look, models and backgrounds
  • Product types and print methods
  • Output sizes for each marketplace and ad format
  • Either as our assisted service, or as a studio application set up for your team

Assisted service. Images are generated with our studio workflow and every image is reviewed for product accuracy before you use it. Results depend on the quality of your references; we do not promise perfect reproduction or unlimited outputs.
