The problem

Every camp office keeps the same figures five times over.

Strength goes into the attendance register, the kitchen indent, the ration account, the daily report and the bills. Each is kept separately, by different people, and they rarely agree. Camp Suite 360 keeps one set of records that every section works from.

Who it is for
  • Training academies and institutes running residential courses
  • Institutional and youth camps
  • Residential workshops and programmes
  • NGO programmes with participants on site
Capabilities

What it does

  • Camp set-up with participant and staff rosters
  • Check-in and daily attendance
  • Daily strength and feeding requirements
  • Training schedules, activities and daily orders
  • Menu planning and ration requirements
  • Stock receipts and issues
  • Procurement records with supporting bills and vendor payments
  • Daily expenditure and budget oversight
  • Accounting reports
  • Medical and other daily registers
  • Shared activity photographs
  • Reports and exports

The benefit

Daily oversight without chasing figures.

The camp commandant or programme head sees strength, feeding, stores, spending and the day's activities in one place — and the final accounts come from the same records.

Camp Suite 360 was first built around NCC camp operations, which bring administration, participant management, messing, stores, procurement and accounts together under tight timelines.
