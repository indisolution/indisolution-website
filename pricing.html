Pricing FAQ

Is the 3-month trial really free?

Yes. Each organisation or department gets three months of Unit Accounts with all features, at no charge. We do not ask for payment details to start.

What happens after the trial?

You can continue on an annual subscription, or stop. If you stop, you can export all your data, and we will hand it over on request.

How are custom solutions priced?

After understanding your requirement, we give a written quote with scope and timeline. The price is agreed before any work starts.
