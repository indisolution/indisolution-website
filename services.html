How we work

Four clear steps

  1. Understand

    We study the process with the people who do the work and agree, in writing, what the software must do.

  2. Build

    We build in small steps and show you working versions as we go.

  3. Train

    We train your staff, hand over simple guides and help move existing records in.

  4. Support

    We stay with you as rules, people and requirements change.

Technology

Proven, low-maintenance foundations

We choose technology that keeps your running costs low and your data under control, rather than the newest thing.

  • Google Workspace and Apps Script — for offices already on Google
  • Cloudflare Workers, cloud storage and databases — fast, low-cost back-ends
  • Python and local applications — where data must stay on your computers
  • Shopify, WhatsApp Cloud API, Meta and Google Ads — for commerce
  • AI models for writing, imagery and document tasks — always with human review
