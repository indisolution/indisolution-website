How we work

Four clear steps

  1. Understand

    We sit with the people who do the work, study the existing files, registers and approvals, and agree what the software must do.

  2. Build

    We build a secure web app around your process, showing you working versions as we go and adjusting on your feedback.

  3. Train

    We train your staff and approving authorities, hand over simple guides and help move existing records in.

  4. Support

    We stay with you — answering questions, fixing issues and making changes as rules, appointments and requirements change.
