Tools & services

Prototype

Visit Diary & Field Reporting

Request a demo

Visit registers are usually written by hand, days later, and are hard to review. Visit Diary captures each visit while it is fresh and produces a consistent page for review and filing.

What it records today

  • Visit date, location, visitor and purpose
  • Activities observed and remarks
  • Supporting photographs
  • Printable A4 diary pages
  • Records saved on the device, with export and import

Where it fits

Institution visits, inspections, field supervision, project monitoring and NGO site visits.

Status: prototype. Cloud sync, Google Drive storage and AI-assisted write-ups can be added as customisation work.

Working app

Service Writer

Request a demo

Official correspondence follows strict formats — headings, spacing, numbering, references and signature blocks. Service Writer takes pasted text or an uploaded document and produces an editable Word file in the prescribed format, every time.

  • Choose the document type
  • Structured originator, addressee, reference and signatory fields
  • Consistent headings, spacing, numbering and signature blocks
  • Frequently used addresses and details saved for reuse
  • Basic text clean-up of spacing, capitals and common slips
  • Editable DOCX output
  • Runs entirely on your computer — no internet service involved

Formats

Current formats include service letters, demi-official letters, service papers, military papers, statements of case and personal applications. The same approach suits government correspondence, institutional letters, office notes, proposals and your own templates.

Status: working application on macOS. Windows and macOS installer packaging is available as a deployment option, subject to testing on your systems.
