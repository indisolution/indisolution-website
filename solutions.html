Custom solutions for organisations

Talk to us

Every organisation has a way of working that has been refined over years — files, registers, sanctions, returns and the chain of approvals. Off-the-shelf software usually asks you to change that. We do the opposite: we study the process and build a secure web application around it.

Typical systems

  • Accounts and fund management
  • Approval workflows with maker-checker control
  • Digital registers (stores, assets, movement, visitors)
  • Returns, reports and periodic statements
  • Dashboards for commanders and heads of office
  • Document generation — letters, notes, certificates

Who it is for

Government departments, armed-forces and NCC units, schools, colleges and institutions, and businesses with a defined process that is still being run on paper or spreadsheets.

Live product

IndiSolution Unit Accounts

Talk to usProduct details

Our flagship, live product. A secure double-entry accounting system designed around how units actually keep accounts — multiple funds, staff entry with commanding-officer approval, and the reports an audit board expects.

  • Regimental, Central Govt, Camp and State Govt funds
  • Maker-checker: staff enter, CO approves
  • Minute sheets and inter-office notes as PDF / Word
  • Bills and quotations attached to every entry
  • Monthly bank reconciliation with statement import
  • Inter-fund loans and camp accounts
  • Quarterly audit-board and surprise-check reports
  • Handing / taking-over certificate between officers

6-month free trial per unit, then an annual subscription.

Unit & organisation monitoring

Talk to us

Commanders and heads of office spend a great deal of time collecting the same figures again and again. A monitoring system brings them together in one place, kept current by the people responsible, with a clear view for those who need to decide.

Typical dashboards

  • Strength and attendance
  • Training progress and certifications
  • Inventory, stores and assets
  • Compliance and pending actions
  • Inspections, observations and closure
  • Exportable returns in your required formats

Each system is custom-built to your units, formats and reporting chain, with role-based access so each level sees what it should.

INDI-chat

Talk to us

INDI-chat is an AI assistant trained on your documents — policies, SOPs, circulars, manuals, product catalogues and FAQs. Staff and customers ask questions in plain language and get answers drawn from your material, with a reference to the source document.

Uses

  • Internal help desk for rules and procedures
  • Customer query handling on your website or WhatsApp
  • Quick search across large document sets
  • Onboarding help for new staff

Your documents remain yours. They are used only to answer your users' questions and are not used to train public AI models.
